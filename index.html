1 / About

About me

I'm about to begin my M.Sc. in Informatics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), following a B.Sc. in Informatics at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). Alongside my studies, I've spent the past few years doing research at the intersection of computer vision and other fields, from panoramic scene understanding to biomedical image analysis.

My goal is to work on research problems in computer vision, particularly segmentation, scene understanding, and methods that help visual models generalize across different domains and environments. I stay open to exploring adjacent areas of the field as new problems come up.

3 / Research Experience

Research experience

02/2026 – Today

Open Panoramic Instance Segmentation

Supervised by Ph.D. student Junwei Zheng at the Computer Vision for Human-Computer Interaction Lab, Institute for Anthropomatics and Robotics (IAR), KIT (Junwei Zheng was also a visiting Ph.D. at the Computer Vision and Geometry Group led by Prof. Dr. Marc Pollefeys at ETH Zurich). A continuation of this project titled "Panoramic Adaptive Sliced Inference (PASI) for Open-Vocabulary Instance Segmentation in Equirectangular Panoramas" was presented at the ECCV 2026 in the Human-Scene Interaction Workshop.

01/2021 – Today

DNA Size Finder

Working in the Institute of Molecular Biology in the Bulgarian Academy of Science on the interdisciplinary computer vision project: "An algorithm and application to efficiently analyse DNA fibre data” with a research supervisor Assoc. Prof. Anastas Gospodinov. At the moment I work on a continuation of this project with the goal of increasing the accuracy of the instance segmentation component in the image processing pipeline. The improved software will be used in the analysis of rare genetic diseases like Rahman Syndrome.

Human-Scene Interaction Workshop · ECCV 2026
